FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is SpendWise free to use?

Yes. Adding expenses, your dashboard, basic reports and insights, default categories, and cloud backup are free for everyone. Pro adds custom categories, CSV/PDF export, and deeper insights.

Do I need to create an account to track expenses?

No. SpendWise is fully usable as a guest with zero sign-up. Create an account later only if you want your expenses backed up to the cloud and synced across devices.

Is my financial data safe and private?

Your expenses live on your device first. If you sign in, backups are protected per-account on our backend — your data is never sold or shared with third parties.

Is SpendWise available on iOS?

SpendWise is available on Android today. iOS is coming soon — check back here for the App Store link.

Which currencies does SpendWise support?

SpendWise supports 50+ currencies from around the world — from the Indian Rupee and US Dollar to the Euro, British Pound, and currencies across the Middle East, Asia Pacific, and Africa. Pick yours during setup and amounts are stored precisely down to the smallest unit, so your totals are always exact.

How is SpendWise different from other expense tracking apps?

Most expense tracking apps go straight to red alerts and shame you for overspending. SpendWise, guided by Penny the mascot, keeps nudges warm and encouraging, logs expenses in a few taps, and never forces you to sign up before you've decided if you like it.

Does SpendWise sync across multiple devices?

Yes. Sign in with email or Google and your expenses back up automatically — free for every account, not just Pro — so they're there when you open the app on another device.
